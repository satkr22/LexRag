109Set off of losses under any other head of income.

Section 109 Â· Chapter VII Â· page 144 of the Act

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Chapter, for any tax year, if income computed under any head of income (other than "Capital gains") is a loss, such loss shall be set off against income of the assessee under any other head, including "Capital gains", if any, assessable for that tax year, subject to the following conditions:â

113Set off and carry forward of losses computed in respect of speculation business.

Section 113 Â· Chapter VII Â· page 148 of the Act

(5) In this section, where any part of the business of the assessee (being a company) consists of purchase and sale of shares of other companies, then the assessee shall be deemed to be carrying on a speculation business, to the extent to which its business consists of purchase and sale of such shares.

(4) For the purposes of this sectionâ
